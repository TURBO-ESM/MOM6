40!> Options selecting the discretization of the Coriolis terms.
41type, public :: Coriolis_scheme_CS
42 integer :: Coriolis_Scheme !< Selects the discretization for the Coriolis terms.
43 !! Valid values are:
44 !! - SADOURNY75_ENERGY - Sadourny, 1975
45 !! - ARAKAWA_HSU90 - Arakawa & Hsu, 1990, Energy & non-div. Enstrophy
46 !! - ROBUST_ENSTRO - Pseudo-enstrophy scheme
47 !! - SADOURNY75_ENSTRO - Sadourny, JAS 1975, Enstrophy
48 !! - ARAKAWA_LAMB81 - Arakawa & Lamb, MWR 1981, Energy & Enstrophy
49 !! - ARAKAWA_LAMB_BLEND - A blend of Arakawa & Lamb with Arakawa & Hsu and Sadourny energy.
50 !! - WENOVI3RD_PV_ENSTRO - 3rd-order WENO scheme for PV reconstruction
51 !! - WENOVI5TH_PV_ENSTRO - 5th-order WENO scheme for PV reconstruction
52 !! - WENOVI7TH_PV_ENSTRO - 7th-order WENO scheme for PV reconstruction
53 !! The default, SADOURNY75_ENERGY, is the safest choice then the
